# What I need from you for the portfolio site

Hey Manish — the site is built and working. I just need your actual stuff to put in it.
You don't need all of this at once; send what's easy first.

---

## 1. Your details (just type these out)

- **Name** — exactly how you want it displayed
- **Date of birth**
- **Phone number** (with country code)
- **Email**
- **Instagram** — profile link
- **YouTube** — channel link
- **LinkedIn** — profile link (skip if you don't use it)

**A one-line tagline.** Something like "Colour · Motion graphics · Short-form" — the 3–4
things you want to be hired for.

**Three lines about you.** Exactly three, one sentence each. First is what you do, second is
what you specialise in, third is anything else (where you're based, who you work with).

---

## 2. Work & education

For each **job**: years (e.g. 2024 – Present), company name, your role.
For each **qualification**: years, institution, what you studied. Self-taught counts —
say so, it reads well.

---

## 3. Your software levels

For each of these, tell me **Expert**, **Advanced**, or **Working**:

DaVinci Resolve · After Effects · Premiere Pro · CapCut · Blender · Lightroom · Canva

Add or remove any — this is just what we've got in there now.

---

## 4. Photos

- **A portrait of you.** Portrait orientation, roughly **3:4** (like 900×1200). Head and
  shoulders works best. It sits against a dark background, so avoid a very dark or very
  busy backdrop.

---

## 5. The showreel — read this bit properly

A **30-second clip**. It plays automatically behind your name the moment someone opens the
site, so it's the single most important asset here.

- **Under 8MB** if you can. 1080p, H.264. If it's bigger, send it anyway and I'll compress.
- **It plays muted** — browsers block sound on autoplay, no way around it. There's an unmute
  button. So it has to work with no audio.
- **Your name and contact should be burned into it** as a watermark. Anything on the internet
  can be screen-recorded; a watermark makes a stolen copy useless to whoever took it.
- **Avoid very bright or very high-contrast footage in these 30 seconds.** Your name and
  contact details sit on top of it. Darker, more evenly-lit footage will look considerably
  better than something with white flashes or hard cuts to bright frames.
- A **still frame** from it as a poster image, if you have one. Otherwise I'll grab one.

---

## 6. Your work

Upload your videos to **YouTube** (they can be **Unlisted** — that means invisible in search
and not on your channel, only reachable from the site link). Then send:

- **Playlist links** for each category: Colour Grade, Short Form Content, Text Tracking
- Any individual videos you want featured

For **client work**: the **Instagram post link** for each, the client's name, and a
thumbnail image for each one.

> **Public vs Unlisted is your call, per video.** Public means people can find your work on
> YouTube on their own, which brings you enquiries. Unlisted means only people you send the
> link to can see it. You can mix — public for your best pieces, unlisted for client work you
> don't own or don't want indexed.

---

## 7. One thing you should know

You'll get a **login for the site**. Once it's live you'll be able to add videos, add clients,
swap your showreel and photo, and update your contact details yourself — no need to ask me.

So don't stress about having every video ready now. Send your details, your photo and the
showreel, and you can fill in the rest whenever.
